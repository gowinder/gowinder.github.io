# The First Sci-Fi Book Haul of the Second Half of This Year Has Arrived


[![DSC04231](http://gowinder.files.wordpress.com/2010/10/dsc04231_thumb.jpg "DSC04231")](http://gowinder.files.wordpress.com/2010/10/dsc04231.jpg)

The batch is Mission of Gravity, The Martian Chronicles and True Names. Mission of Gravity uses that cover — and wow, that illustration is from some old issue of Science Fiction World. Even back then I was wondering how a plane like that flies. No engine — is it a glider? No idea whether it has anything to do with the novel. Word is this book is hard core. So I bought it.

The Martian Chronicles — there's also that other "Mars trilogy", the Red Mars sort of thing. That set looks incomprehensible going by the reviews, so much so that I didn't want to read any Mars book at all. In the end I saw a review on Douban saying this one was okay, so I picked it up. Damn you, Douban.

True Names was a must. I lost that one in the last haul, can't forget it this time, otherwise it'll be out of stock again. Sigh — when will I ever get my hands on A Deepness in the Sky and A Fire Upon the Deep?

These few were bought at Xinhua Bookstore, and a few more from Joyo, probably arriving in the next couple of days. I'll read them one by one, and write reviews when I'm done.
