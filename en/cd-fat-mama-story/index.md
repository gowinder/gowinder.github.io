# CD: Fat Mama's Story


![Fat Mama's Story](https://img1.doubanio.com/view/subject/m/public/s1443060.jpg)
I used to always download APEs and burn my own discs, but this time was different — by chance I heard a song called "Flowers on the Sea", and I bought the CD without a second thought!

The singer is Maria. Honestly, I'd never even heard the name before, and she performs this song to perfection — I think it's spot on. That understated mood, a touch of melancholy, passion inside plainness — maybe that's exactly what it's like to live in an ordinary world, a kind of self-intoxication. Intoxicated by what? Listen to the song and you'll find out: any little thing, as long as you savour it carefully, will always yield a trace of delight.

This song is best listened to alone, at dusk, with the lights off and a light rain outside, raindrops sliding gently down the window glass, while you lie lazily on the sofa with a glass of red wine beside you and take an occasional sip. Mmm.....~~~~ I wonder when I'll get a chance like that. The time, the place....
