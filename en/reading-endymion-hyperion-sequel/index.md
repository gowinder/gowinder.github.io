# Starting the Hyperion Sequel, Endymion


The full set of original editions arrived right after New Year, and I never got a chance to read them. I'd meant to start from the first book, but I found the plot had lost its freshness, and reading it in English made it hard to keep going, so I decided to start straight from the sequel.

Two days in, I've read two pages and run into a pile of unfamiliar words. Still, it's not too bad — without a dictionary I can more or less guess the plot, and with a dictionary I get it properly. But if you asked me to translate the whole thing into Chinese, I don't think I'm up to it. Right now I'm stuck at the beginner stage of "getting the gist".

I've got big ambitions though: if I can actually finish these two books, then I'll never have to wait for Chinese translations of sci-fi again — I can just order the newest originals from Blue Spring or grab ebooks online. Wouldn't that put me way out in front?![](http://img.baidu.com/hi/jx2/j_0011.gif)

Here's the cover, which I really like. Original-edition covers are just pleasant to look at; domestic covers are all stitched together on a computer, which is so boring.

![](http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/fd/Endymion_cover.jpg)

The one covered in spikes all over is the famous future killer, the Shrike. The one rowing the boat on the left and the blue-skinned one, I don't know them. The small woman on the right is probably Rachel, who got un-aged — by the end of the second book she had already regressed into a baby and was close to dying. From the cover it looks like she survived later on, but I haven't got that far yet.

Endymion is written in first person by the character Endymion, and the story takes place 247 years after the second book.

How the survival of humans and AIs actually turns out — I'll keep reading to find out.
