Deploy a Local Stable Diffusion Runtime Environment

midjourney has been quite popular lately, but it has started charging—10 dollars a month, which is more than I can afford—so I switched to the open-source Stable Diffusion. You can deploy it on Google Colab with one click: https://github.com/camenduru/stable-diffusion-webui-colab. However, the free gpu on Google Colab runs out quickly, and although the paid one is cheap, you still have to use it sparingly. If you have a local gpu with more than 8G of VRAM, you can run it on your own computer to save costs; 6G of VRAM should also work—try lowering the resolution and keywords.
