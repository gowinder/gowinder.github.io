# About


Welcome. This is a personal blog — a place where I keep notes on coding, tinkering, and the occasional non-technical aside.

## What is here

Most posts are things I ran into while building software: backend services, self-hosting and home-lab experiments, the odd front-end snag, and notes on AI tooling. Some are long write-ups; some are two-line reminders to my future self. They are written the way I would explain them to a colleague — what broke, why, and what actually fixed it.

## What I build

Alongside writing, I build small single-purpose things and run them in public:

- **[eldertongue.com](https://eldertongue.com)** — an Old English dictionary, organised for people actually reading Old English texts rather than browsing an archive.
- **[massrename.com](https://massrename.com)** — a batch file renamer that works in the browser: stack rules, preview every result, then run it once.

More on the [projects page](/en/projects/).
