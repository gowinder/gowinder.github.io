# My Dopod Phone Got Stolen — So Depressed!


It got stolen yesterday while I was changing to the 527 at Xinhua Road. Ugh — I'd only paid 1 instalment, and there are 23 more to go. Ugh!

It's all because I was too sleepy. All I could think about was getting on and grabbing a seat. The guy in front was so obviously covering for the guy behind him, and I was still set on squeezing past!

From now on I can't be groggy when I get on the bus — find a seat first, then sleep!

No more fanny pack either.

Backpack from now on.

Damn it, my buddy's wallet got stolen during the day and I'd even reminded myself to be careful.

Fellow sufferers in this world, eh.

I'll just treat it as losing money to ward off worse luck!

My D600 — it was so great to use, and it was a birthday present from my wife! Ugh!

A lesson paid for in blood!
