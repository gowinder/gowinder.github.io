# (500) Days of Summer


![(500) Days of Summer](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/M/MV5BMTk5MjM4OTU1OV5BMl5BanBnXkFtZTcwODkzNDIzMw@@._V1_FMjpg_UX1000_.jpg)


Today I watched a movie with my wife called "(500) Days of Summer". I'd never seen the male lead, but the female lead is the one from Yes Man, Zooey Deschanel — she has a lot of that America's Sweetheart Meg Ryan feel from back in the day. In this film she's the same, all quirky and sprite-like.  
The plot isn't cheap either: the ending isn't a happy-ever-after, but it isn't a tragedy either. It's just like life — a relationship ends painfully, and that ending is itself a new beginning.  
  
The most memorable thing is the soundtrack. Different scenes, different music, and it fits beautifully; the rhythm tracks the story as it unfolds. My personal favourite is Kevin Michael's [At Last (Bonus Track)](http://www.xiami.com/song/1769359034) — very bluesy, very jazzy. Jazz really is still my favourite.
